Contact Companion Privacy Policy

Updated: September 27, 2026

Contact Companion runs locally and helps you manage contacts, visit records, tasks, birthdays and seasonal reminders. The app does not provide transactions, payments, brokerage services or a centralized customer management backend.

1. Default data processing

Contacts, household relationships, visit records, notes, tasks and reminders you create are stored in the app's sandbox on your current device by default, protected by system secure storage and local encryption. The app has no built-in cloud synchronization and does not upload this data to the developer's servers. You may use system sharing to save a backup you explicitly export to iCloud or another location you choose. The app does not use advertising tracking or cross-app tracking.

2. Optional permissions

  • Camera: used only when you choose to photograph information.
  • Photos: used only when you choose or save an image.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: used only when you record a voice memo or use voice input. Apple speech recognition processes transcription and may send audio to Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy. This processing is independent of the custom AI setting. You can enter text manually if you decline permission.
  • Contacts: the system picker returns only the contacts you select; the app does not request full address book access. After confirmation, names, all phone numbers and email addresses, company and available Gregorian birthdays are saved locally. Birthdays retain only the month and day, not the birth year, and import does not automatically enable birthday reminders. Import neither uploads contacts nor modifies the system address book.
  • Apple speech privacy information: Siri, Dictation & Privacy.
  • Notifications: used only for task, contact service, and contact or family birthday reminders you enable. Contact birthday reminders must be enabled separately, and you control system notification permission. Disabling notifications retains reminder settings. When you enable them again or restore a backup, valid reminders are rescheduled using local settings. Birthday notifications do not show contact names on the lock screen.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: used to verify your identity when opening the app only after you enable app lock. Verification is performed by the system. The app does not obtain or store your facial data, fingerprints or device passcode.

3. User-configured AI services

The app does not include or proxy a generative AI service. Only after you enter your own service URL, model name and private access key, and separately enable data transmission consent and AI assistance, will the app send selected text, speech transcripts or images to that endpoint. The automated assistant uses only unarchived contacts with individually recorded third-party AI authorization, selecting the necessary names, summaries, relationships, interactions and progress for the task. Unauthorized information can still be processed locally. Before input parsing sends data, you must also confirm that the selected information has the required authorization. You may withdraw authorization in a contact's details or disable data processing permission and AI assistance in the custom AI settings.

You choose the endpoint. Before enabling it, review the provider, its location and its privacy terms. The provider may retain inputs and generated results; its policies govern their use, retention and deletion. This app cannot delete information already sent to that provider on its behalf. Your private access key is kept in the local Keychain and is excluded from exported backups. Importing a backup does not change the local service URL, key, or AI enablement and authorization settings.

4. Place lookup

If you enter a place and select date correction, the app uses Apple's geocoding service to convert the place text into longitude and a time zone. Results are used only for local birthday and seasonal reminder calculations and are stored locally.

5. Backups, export and deletion

You may create a password-encrypted backup. If you choose a plain JSON export, that file is not protected by a backup password. Backups may contain contacts and records; you choose a secure location to save, share or delete them. You can view, edit, export, archive or delete records in the app. Archiving does not delete a record. Uninstalling the app removes its sandbox data, but keys in the system Keychain may remain; clear the custom AI configuration before uninstalling. Exported backups and copies shared with others must be deleted separately.

6. Policy updates and contact

If data processing changes, we will update this page's date and content. You can read this policy offline in Settings → Privacy policy. For privacy or data deletion questions, email zhang_apple1989@sina.com. You can delete data in the app yourself. For information already sent to a custom endpoint, contact the relevant service provider.
