Client Network by ZW support

Client Network by ZW is an iPhone app for contacts, visits, tasks and reminders. For help, email zhang_apple1989@sina.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, steps and any error message. Do not send real contact details, backup files or access keys.

Where are records stored?

By default, records stay on the current device. Export a backup yourself and keep it safely before uninstalling. Automatic cloud sync is not provided.

How do I restore a backup?

Open Settings > Import & export, select your backup, and follow the instructions to replace or merge records. Replacement removes current records, so back them up first. You need the original password for an encrypted backup.

What if saving fails?

Contact, conversation and task forms keep your input. Check available device storage and try again. Keep the form open after a failed save and do not uninstall the app. Export password-protected backups regularly.

How do I add a contact or family birthday?

Edit the contact or family member, enter the Gregorian month and day, and enable the 09:00 birthday reminder if desired. A birth year is not needed. Settings > Contact and family birthdays lists upcoming dates. February 29 is observed on February 28 in non-leap years.

Why are reminders missing after a restore?

A restore rechecks valid reminders and removes old reminders for replaced records. Check the app notification setting and the iPhone system permission. Denied permission prevents delivery. Allow notifications again and return to the app to refill the schedule. The nearest 60 reminders are scheduled first; later items remain in the app and are filled in when you open it.

Can merging a backup duplicate contacts?

Contacts with the same identifier, or a matching name and uniquely matching phone/email, are merged. Notes, all contact methods and linked records are retained. Local authorization and reminder choices take priority. Different names sharing a phone number, or ambiguous matches, are kept separate to prevent a wrong merge.

How do I change the interface language?

Open Settings > Preferences > Language and choose English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese or the system language. The change takes effect immediately and is saved. Contact names, notes and existing records keep their original text.

Does language selection change the currency?

No. Amounts are displayed in Chinese yuan (CNY). Choosing a language changes presentation and dates, without converting stored amounts.

Does custom AI cost extra?

Core records, backups and reminders do not require AI. Custom AI is off by default. If you use your own provider, it may charge separately; check its pricing and privacy terms first.

How do I turn off AI assistance?

In Settings > Custom AI service, turn off assistance and data processing permission. The app does not enable a custom service by default. Apple speech recognition may process audio online independently of this switch.

How do I delete data?

Delete records in the app. Exported backups must be deleted separately where you saved them. For information already sent to a custom service, contact that provider. Read the privacy policy for details.
